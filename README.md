## What I'm building
**ChurnPit** is the product I'm currently building.

Many businesses lose customers without ever knowing why, or when.
ChurnPit uses AI to watch your customer data and emails you a
report with the customers most at risk of leaving, so you can act
early. There is no dashboard to check.

The waitlist is open, and early members get 50% off for as long
as their subscription is active.
[Join the ChurnPit waitlist](https://churnpit.orviton.com)
